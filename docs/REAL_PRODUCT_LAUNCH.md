## First application: Anker through Impact

1. Open the official [Anker affiliate page](https://www.anker.com/become-an-affiliate).
2. Choose the Impact **Get Started** option. The official application target is
   `https://app.impact.com/campaign-campaign-info-v2/Anker.brand`.
3. Create or sign in to an Impact publisher account.
4. Use `Workspace Signal` as the public property name and
   `https://workspacesignal.com` as the website.
5. Use the legal individual name only in private identity, tax, and payment
   fields. It does not need to replace the public Workspace Signal brand.
6. Paste this property description:

   > Workspace Signal is an independent US-focused editorial publication about
   > home-office and workspace upgrades. We publish original buying guides,
   > product comparisons, compatibility notes, and price-aware recommendations.
   > Distribution uses organic search and organic Pinterest content that links
   > to our claimed website. We do not use misleading claims, cookie stuffing,
   > trademark impersonation, or direct paid-search links.

7. Declare the website and the Workspace Signal Pinterest account as promotional
   properties when the application permits both.
8. Read and accept the current contract only if its traffic, disclosure,
   Pinterest, redirect, and media rules match the actual operation.

Anker's official page currently publishes an 8% rate, a 30-day cookie, data
feeds, custom deep links, and eligibility for individuals and social publishers.
The signed Impact contract controls if it differs.

## Activation gate

Do not label a link affiliate-enabled until all are true:

- written program approval is received;
- current contract and rate are recorded;
- payout and tax setup is complete;
- Pinterest and website placement rules are confirmed;
- image/media rights are confirmed;
- the exact tracking link passes a test click;
- the page contains a nearby disclosure;
- the product price and availability have been rechecked.
