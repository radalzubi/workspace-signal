# Compliance checklist

- Affiliate disclosure is conspicuous, understandable, and uses the provider’s
  exact required language.
- Platform commercial-content and AI disclosure fields are configured where
  required.
- Every product/media source permits commercial reuse; authorization metadata is
  recorded. No copyrighted music is downloaded from social platforms.
- Claims come only from supplied facts and observed data. No invented ratings,
  fake endorsements, personal-use claims, guarantees, or unsupported superlatives.
- “Observed low” is never represented as “lowest ever.” Price and availability are
  revalidated before publication.
- Tracking is minimized and documented; consent/cookie requirements are assessed
  for the target country; deletion/retention procedures exist.
- Advertising is official, disclosed, budget-capped, evidence-gated, and contains
  no artificial engagement.
- Program/platform terms are reviewed again before enabling a new provider,
  country, content format, redirect method, or social adapter.

This checklist supports operations but is not jurisdiction-specific legal advice.
